Waves and Optics

Sound, Light, Reflection, Refraction

Core wave formula

v = fλ

Where v is wave speed, f is frequency and λ is wavelength.

Reflection and refraction

  • Reflection occurs when light bounces off a surface.
  • Refraction occurs when light changes speed and direction in a different medium.
  • In a mirror, the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection.

Worked example

A sound wave has frequency 440 Hz and wavelength 0.75 m. Find the speed.

Solution:

Use v = fλ

v = 440 × 0.75 = 330 m/s

Answer: 330 m/s
