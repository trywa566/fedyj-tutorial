Coordinate Geometry

Points, Lines and Slopes

Definitions

Coordinate geometry connects algebra and geometry by using coordinates on a graph.

Gradient m = (y₂ - y₁)/(x₂ - x₁)
Distance = √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]
Equation of a straight line: y = mx + c

Worked example

Find the gradient between points (2, 3) and (6, 11).

Solution:

m = (11 - 3)/(6 - 2) = 8/4 = 2

Answer: gradient = 2
