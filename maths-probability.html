Probability

Chance and Measurement

Fundamental definition

P(event) = number of favourable outcomes / total number of outcomes

Worked example

A fair die is rolled once. What is the probability of getting a 4?

Solution: There are 6 possible outcomes: 1,2,3,4,5,6. Only one is favourable.

P(4) = 1/6
