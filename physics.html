Physics

Form 1 → Upper Sixth

Core physics areas

Physics covers measurement, motion, forces, energy, heat, sound, light, electricity, magnetism, atomic structure and modern physics.

  • Form 1–3: measurements, force, pressure, heat and simple electricity.
  • Form 4–5: motion, energy, waves, light, circuits, magnetism, radioactivity.
  • Lower/Upper Sixth: mechanics, fields, oscillations, optics, electromagnetic induction and quantum physics.

Mechanics

Motion, force, Newton’s laws, work, power and energy.

Electricity

Current, resistance, Ohm’s law, power and circuit calculations.

Waves and Optics

Wave properties, sound, light, reflection, refraction and lenses.
