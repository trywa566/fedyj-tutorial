Atomic & Nuclear Physics

Atoms, Radiation and Radioactivity

Key ideas

  • Atoms contain a nucleus of protons and neutrons.
  • Electrons move around the nucleus.
  • Radioactive decay can emit alpha, beta and gamma radiation.

Worked example

What is the difference between alpha and beta radiation?

Solution: Alpha radiation is a helium nucleus with two protons and two neutrons. Beta radiation is a fast-moving electron. They differ in mass, charge and penetrating power.
