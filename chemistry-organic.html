Organic Chemistry

Hydrocarbons, Series and Reactions

Core ideas

Organic chemistry studies compounds containing carbon. Hydrocarbons are compounds made of hydrogen and carbon only.

  • Alkanes are saturated hydrocarbons.
  • Alkenes contain a double bond.
  • Alcohols contain the -OH group.

Worked example

What is the empirical formula of ethane, C₂H₆?

Solution: Divide both numbers by 2: C₁H₃.

Answer: CH₃
