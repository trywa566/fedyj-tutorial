Magnetism

Fields, Induction and Electromagnets

Important facts

  • Like poles repel and unlike poles attract.
  • Magnetic field lines move from north to south outside a magnet.
  • Electric current can produce a magnetic field.

Worked example

Why does a current-carrying wire generate a magnetic field?

Solution: Moving electric charges create a magnetic field around the conductor. This is the basis of electromagnets and electric motors.
