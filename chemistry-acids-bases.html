Acids and Bases

pH, Neutralisation, Salts

Definitions

  • Acid: substance that releases hydrogen ions in solution.
  • Base: substance that reacts with acids to form salt and water.
  • Alkali: a soluble base.
Acid + Base → Salt + Water

pH scale

The pH scale tells us how acidic or alkaline a solution is.

  • pH < 7 = acid
  • pH = 7 = neutral
  • pH > 7 = alkali

Worked example

What is formed when hydrochloric acid reacts with sodium hydroxide?

Solution:

HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O

So the products are sodium chloride and water.
