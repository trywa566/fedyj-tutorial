Heat

Temperature, Expansion and Transfer

Basic concepts

Heat is a form of energy that flows from hot to cold objects. Temperature measures how hot or cold a body is.

Q = mcΔT

Q is heat energy, m is mass, c is specific heat capacity and ΔT is change in temperature.

Methods of heat transfer

  • Conduction in solids.
  • Convection in liquids and gases.
  • Radiation through empty space.
