Types of Triangles

Form 1–5

Definition

A triangle is a three-sided polygon. The three interior angles always add up to 180°.

Angle sum of a triangle: A + B + C = 180°

Types of triangles

Type Definition Key property
EquilateralAll three sides equalAll angles are 60°
IsoscelesTwo sides equalBase angles are equal
ScaleneNo equal sidesAll angles are different
AcuteAll angles less than 90°All acute
RightOne angle is 90°Uses Pythagoras theorem
ObtuseOne angle greater than 90°One obtuse angle

Important formulas

Perimeter = sum of all sides
Area = 1/2 × base × height
Pythagoras: a² + b² = c²

Worked example 1

A triangle has angles 50° and 70°. Find the third angle.

Solution:

Use the triangle angle sum rule: A + B + C = 180°.

So, C = 180° - 50° - 70° = 60°.

Answer: 60°

Worked example 2

A right triangle has two sides 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

Solution:

Use Pythagoras theorem: a² + b² = c².

6² + 8² = c²

36 + 64 = c² = 100

c = √100 = 10 cm

Answer: 10 cm
