Optics

Light, Mirrors, Lenses and Refraction

Basic laws

  • Angle of incidence = angle of reflection
  • Light refracts because its speed changes in different media.

Worked example

If the angle of incidence is 35°, what is the angle of reflection?

Solution: In reflection, the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection.

Answer: 35°
