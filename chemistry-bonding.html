Chemical Bonding

Ionic, Covalent and Metallic

Bonding basics

Chemical bonding occurs when atoms join together to form compounds. Atoms bond to achieve a more stable electron arrangement.

  • Ionic bonding occurs between metals and non-metals.
  • Covalent bonding occurs mainly between non-metals.
  • Metallic bonding occurs in metals.

Worked example

What type of bond forms between sodium and chlorine?

Solution: Sodium is a metal and chlorine is a non-metal. This forms an ionic bond because electrons transfer from sodium to chlorine. Sodium becomes Na⁺ and chlorine becomes Cl⁻. The charges attract and form sodium chloride, NaCl.
