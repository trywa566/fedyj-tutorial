Circles

Geometry

Key facts

A circle is a set of points that are all the same distance from the centre.

Circumference = 2πr
Area = πr²
Arc length = θ/360 × 2πr
Sector area = θ/360 × πr²

Important parts of a circle

  • Centre: middle point of the circle.
  • Radius: distance from centre to circumference.
  • Diameter: straight line through centre from one side to the other.
  • Chord: straight line joining two points on the circumference.
  • Tangent: a straight line touching the circle at one point.

Worked example

A circle has radius 7 cm. Find the circumference and area.

Solution:

Use C = 2πr and A = πr².

C = 2 × π × 7 = 14π cm ≈ 43.98 cm

A = π × 7² = 49π cm² ≈ 153.94 cm²

Answer: circumference ≈ 43.98 cm and area ≈ 153.94 cm²
