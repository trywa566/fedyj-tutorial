Preparation of Common Compounds

HCl, NH₃, NaOH and salts

Hydrochloric acid

Hydrogen chloride gas is prepared by reaction between sodium chloride and concentrated sulfuric acid.

NaCl + H₂SO₄ → NaHSO₄ + HCl

In water, hydrogen chloride dissolves to form hydrochloric acid.

Ammonia

Ammonia is prepared by heating ammonium salts with an alkali.

NH₄Cl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O + NH₃

Sodium hydroxide

Sodium hydroxide is commonly produced by the chlor-alkali process.

It is important in neutralisation and precipitation reactions.
