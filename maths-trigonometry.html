Trigonometry

Right Triangle Ratios

Main ratios

In a right-angle triangle:

sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse
cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse
tan θ = opposite / adjacent

Why these formulas work

They come from the relationship between the sides of a right triangle and the angle at the point where the triangle is measured.

Use the side opposite the angle, the side adjacent to it, and the hypotenuse.

Worked example

In a right triangle, the angle is 30°, the opposite side is 5 cm, and the hypotenuse is unknown. Find the hypotenuse.

Solution: Use sin 30° = opposite / hypotenuse.

0.5 = 5 / h

h = 5 / 0.5 = 10 cm

Answer: 10 cm
