Electrolysis

Ions, Electrolytes and Products

Definition

Electrolysis is the decomposition of an electrolyte by passing an electric current through it.

  • Cathode is the negative electrode.
  • Anode is the positive electrode.

Worked example

During the electrolysis of molten sodium chloride, what products are formed at the electrodes?

Solution: At the cathode, sodium ions are reduced to sodium metal. At the anode, chloride ions are oxidised to chlorine gas.

Product summary: sodium at cathode and chlorine at anode.
