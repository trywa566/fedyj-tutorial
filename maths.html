Mathematics

Form 1 → Upper Sixth

Core mathematics areas

Mathematics covers arithmetics, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, coordinate geometry, statistics, probability and calculus.

  • Form 1–3: number work, measure, basic equations, geometry and graphs.
  • Form 4–5: algebraic manipulation, circle theorems, trigonometry, statistics and coordinate geometry.
  • Lower/Upper Sixth: calculus, vectors, functions, probability and advanced geometry.
