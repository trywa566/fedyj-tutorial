Mathematics

Form 1 → Upper Sixth

Core maths areas

Mathematics in the Cameroon GCEB pathway includes number work, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics, vectors, calculus and mechanics-related mathematics.

  • Form 1: number systems, ratios, basic geometry, measures, simple algebra.
  • Form 2: more algebra, angle geometry, transformations, area and volume.
  • Form 3: equations, graphs, trigonometry, statistics, powers and indices.
  • Form 4/5: quadratic equations, circles, locus, probability, coordinate geometry, trigonometric ratios.
  • Lower/Upper Sixth: calculus, sequences, vectors, conic sections, probability distributions and advanced geometry.

Types of Triangles

Equilateral, isosceles, scalene, acute, obtuse and right triangles. Angle sum and Pythagoras.

Circles

Radius, diameter, chord, tangent, arc, sector, angle theorems and area calculations.

Algebra

Expansions, factorisation, linear equations, quadratic equations and index laws.
