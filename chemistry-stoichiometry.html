Stoichiometry

Mole concept and equations

Key idea

Stoichiometry is the calculation of quantities in chemical reactions. It depends on balanced chemical equations.

moles = mass / molar mass
mass = moles × molar mass

Worked example

Find the moles in 18 g of water, H₂O. Relative molecular mass of water is 18 g/mol.

moles = 18 / 18 = 1 mol

Answer: 1 mol
