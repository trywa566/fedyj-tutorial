Electricity

Current, Resistance, Power

Main formulas

Current = charge / time
Ohm’s law: V = IR
Power = VI = I²R = V²/R

Definitions

  • Current is the flow of charge.
  • Voltage is the potential difference across a component.
  • Resistance opposes current flow.

Worked example

A resistor has a potential difference of 12 V across it and a current of 3 A. Find the resistance.

Solution:

Use V = IR.

R = V / I = 12 / 3 = 4 Ω

Answer: 4 Ω
